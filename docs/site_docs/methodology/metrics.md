# Evaluation Metrics, Statistical Validation & Grad-CAM Interpretability

This section documents the mathematical formulations for **Equations (6) through (12)** from Section II.D and Section III.B.2 of the JEEEMI article.

---

## 1. Quantitative Classification Metrics — Equations (6) to (9)

Let $TP$ denote True Positives, $TN$ denote True Negatives, $FP$ denote False Positives, and $FN$ denote False Negatives for a given diagnostic class. The four standard evaluation metrics are formally defined in **Equations (6)–(9)** of the JEEEMI article:

$$
\text{Accuracy} = \frac{TP + TN}{TP + TN + FP + FN} \tag{6}
$$

$$
\text{Precision} = \frac{TP}{TP + FP} \tag{7}
$$

$$
\text{Recall} = \frac{TP}{TP + FN} \tag{8}
$$

$$
\text{F1-Score} = 2 \cdot \frac{\text{Precision} \cdot \text{Recall}}{\text{Precision} + \text{Recall}} \tag{9}
$$

### Attack Success Rate (ASR)

The **Attack Success Rate (ASR)** quantifies the percentage of test instances misclassified by the model under adversarial perturbation:

$$
\text{ASR (\%)} = 100\% - \text{Accuracy}_{\text{adv}}\text{ (\%)}
$$

---

## 2. Statistical Significance Validation (95% CI & McNemar's Test)

To verify that observed accuracy drops are statistically deterministic rather than sampling artifacts ($N = 2,117$), two statistical procedures are applied:

1. **95% Confidence Intervals (Exact Binomial Clopper-Pearson Limits):** Computes the 95% CI bounds for classification accuracy on Clean, FGSM, and PGD test sets. Complete non-overlap between the lower bound of the Clean CI ($94.53\%$) and the upper bounds of the FGSM CI ($61.37\%$) and PGD CI ($27.12\%$) confirms severe performance collapse.
2. **McNemar's Test with Continuity Correction ($\chi^2$):** Evaluated on paired nominal predictions (Clean vs. FGSM and Clean vs. PGD):

$$
\chi^2 = \frac{(|b - c| - 1)^2}{b + c}
$$

where $b$ is the number of instances correctly classified on clean data but misclassified under attack, and $c$ is the number of instances misclassified on clean data but correctly classified under attack.

---

## 3. Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) — Equations (10) & (11)

We complement quantitative metrics with qualitative and spatial interpretability using **Grad-CAM** applied to the final convolutional layer of the DenseNet-121 architecture (`model.features[-1]` / `features.denseblock4.denselayer16.conv2`).

Let $A^k \in \mathbb{R}^{u \times v}$ represent the $k$-th feature map activation of the final convolutional layer, and let $y^c$ denote the predicted score (logit) for target class $c$. Neuron importance weights $\alpha_k^c$ are computed by globally averaging the gradients of $y^c$ with respect to feature map pixels $A_{ij}^k$ as defined in **Equation (10)** of the JEEEMI article:

$$
\alpha_k^c = \frac{1}{Z} \sum_{i} \sum_{j} \frac{\partial y^c}{\partial A_{ij}^k} \tag{10}
$$

where $Z = u \times v$ is the total number of spatial pixels in the feature map ($7 \times 7 = 49$). The final spatial localization map $L_{\text{Grad-CAM}}^c$ is generated by applying a $\text{ReLU}$ activation to the weighted linear combination of forward activation maps (**Equation 11** of the JEEEMI article):

$$
L_{\text{Grad-CAM}}^c = \text{ReLU}\left(\sum_{k} \alpha_k^c A^k\right) \tag{11}
$$

The $\text{ReLU}$ operation ensures that only features exerting a positive influence on the target class prediction $c$ are highlighted.

---

## 4. Quantifying Spatial Attention Shift via Cosine Similarity ($S_c$) — Equation (12)

To mathematically quantify the degree of semantic retention versus spatial attention displacement (*Semantic Decoupling*), we compute the **Cosine Similarity ($S_c$)** between the flattened 1D Grad-CAM activation vectors of the clean image ($M_{\text{clean}}$) and the perturbed image ($M_{\text{adv}}$), as defined in **Equation (12)** of the JEEEMI article:

$$
S_c = \frac{M_{\text{clean}} \cdot M_{\text{adv}}}{\|M_{\text{clean}}\| \, \|M_{\text{adv}}\|} \tag{12}
$$

### Interpretation of $S_c$ Regimes

- **$S_c \approx 0.79$ (Semantic Inversion Regime):** High spatial correlation indicates that the model's attention remains anchored within pulmonary boundaries, but adversarial noise synthesizes deceptive local textures inside the ROI that invert the predicted pathology (e.g., *COVID-19* misclassified as *Viral Pneumonia*).
- **$S_c \ll 0.50$ (Semantic Decoupling Regime):** Low spatial correlation ($S_c = 0.32$ for *Lung Opacity*, $S_c = 0.38$ for *Normal*) proves mathematical divergence in attention, where focus shifts away from pulmonary parenchyma toward spurious background edge artifacts.
